# Kinematic post-weld joining

Date: 2026-11-13. `WELD JOIN child parent` is an explicit scene operation after `WELD OFF`. Its purpose is to make a completed seam actionable in the browser workcell. It reparents the named child model to the parent model with the child's world pose preserved. This is a kinematic relationship only: it does not compute a physical bond, heat, weld strength, or contact forces.

The parser and planner require a completed `WELD ON object` → LIN/CIRC seam → `WELD OFF` interval involving either `child` or `parent`. A seam authorization is consumed by one JOIN; a later JOIN needs another completed seam. JOIN is forbidden during an active seam and rejects identical child and parent names. At JOIN time, the TCP must be within 0.040 m of **both** named models' collision surfaces. A remote child or parent produces line-numbered `WELD_JOIN_DISTANCE` with both measured distances. The detached collision scene then calls `attach(child, 'model/parent', q)`, checks the resulting assembly for collision, and rejects missing objects or cycles. Joined assembly parts may touch internally while still being checked against the robot and outside objects.

A joined child cannot be joined again or pulled away with `GRAB child` or `RELEASE child`. Preflight emits `WELD_ALREADY_JOINED`, `GRAB_JOINED`, or `RELEASE_JOINED` at the offending source line. `RELEASE` also requires a robot-held object. The intended handling is to grab and release the assembly parent; there is no DETACH or CUT command yet. A child held directly by the robot may still be joined to a model because JOIN preserves its world pose and changes its parent deliberately.

The planned motion result adds `{time,kind:'WELD_JOIN',object:child,child,parent,frame:'model/parent',line}` to `plan.events`. The same join is recorded in `plan.weld.events` and `plan.processTrajectory.events` with a TCP pose. At that event time, `ProgramPlayer` calls `SceneModel.attach(child,'model/parent',{keepWorldPose:true})`. A later `GRAB parent` attaches the entire nested assembly to the TCP. `RELEASE parent` leaves the child joined to the parent. `Reset` reloads the original scene snapshot and restores its initial attachment records, so a program that began unjoined returns to that state. The Workcell objects list displays `child → model/parent` while joined.

The orange seam preview is attached to the named workpiece for rendering. Reset replaces the scene model group; the Workcell UI recreates the preview on the new group. The Chromium regression checks that the preview is still attached after Reset.

## Runnable two-plate example

[`testowe/weld/world.sdf`](../testowe/weld/world.sdf) contains a grey `workpiece` and copper `extension_plate`, initially separate, with collision and visual boxes. Their faces meet at x=0.65 m. The seam endpoint is x=0.65 m, z=0; both plate surfaces are 0.025 m away, inside the 0.040 m gate. The Cartesian [`robot.urdf`](../testowe/weld/robot.urdf) is shared with the weld-path example. [`join_program.txt`](../testowe/weld/join_program.txt) approaches, traces a CIRC and LIN seam on `workpiece`, joins `extension_plate`, pauses, grabs the parent, carries both, and releases.

The Chromium Worker probe [`notes/scripts/check_weld_join.cjs`](scripts/check_weld_join.cjs) loads these files in the standalone app. Its archived [JSON](artifacts/weld_join.json), [joined view](artifacts/weld_join_attached.png), and [carried view](artifacts/weld_join_carried.png) record the results. Planning generated four moves in 5.6075 s and 2,061 timed samples with no diagnostics, collision issues, or page errors. JOIN occurs at 3.9576 s, GRAB at 4.1576 s, RELEASE at 5.6075 s. At JOIN, the child remains at (0.75, 0.05, -0.075) m and the Workcell list reads `extension_plate → model/workpiece`. After parent motion and release, parent and child have both moved by approximately (-0.35, -0.24, 0) m; child remains attached to parent. Reset restores parent (0.35, 0.05, -0.075) m and child (0.75, 0.05, -0.075) m with no attachments.

The same browser probe downloads a project ZIP midway through carry, when both `extension_plate → model/workpiece` and `workpiece → robot/seam_cartesian/link/tcp_link` are active. It also downloads one after RELEASE. Reopening each ZIP through the Workcell file input preserves its attachment hierarchy, both world poses, and the program text. The carried ZIP restores both nested links; the released ZIP restores only the child-to-parent link. The Workcell list reflects each state.

Project ZIPs now store independent robot joint values and restore them before attachments; see [joint-state persistence](2026-11-13_project_joint_state.md). Old ZIPs without that entry still load at URDF home. Playback time is not archived.

`tests/weld-join-fixture.test.cjs` repeats the full Chromium Worker/playback/reset/two-ZIP check. `tests/weld-plan.test.cjs` checks remote child, already joined child, GRAB/RELEASE joined child, and JOIN/GRAB/RELEASE event order, while `tests/program.test.cjs` checks grammar and seam consumption. `tests/program-player.test.cjs` directly checks nested attachment movement and reset.
