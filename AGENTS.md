Read [`notes/2026-11-13_workcell_roadmap.md`](notes/2026-11-13_workcell_roadmap.md) before changing the workcell implementation. It is the decision ledger and task checklist for this project. Update its status and evidence whenever a task is completed or a design decision changes.
